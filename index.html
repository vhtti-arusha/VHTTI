About VHTTI

The VETA Hotel and Tourism Training Institute (VHTTI) is a vocational training institution established to provide quality technical and vocational education in hospitality and tourism, preparing skilled professionals for the needs of the labour market.

Institutional Background

VHTTI traces its history to its establishment and registration in 2011 under registration number REG/BTP/001VHTTI. In 2019, the Institute received accreditation from the National Council for Technical Education (NACTE), now the National Council for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (NACTVET), enabling it to conduct training up to National Technical Award (NTA) Level Six.

Initially, the Institute offered Hospitality programmes in Culinary Arts, Rooms Division, and Food and Beverage Service and Sales at NTA Levels 4, 5 and 6. In 2018, it expanded into competency-based Tourism programmes, including Tour Guiding (NTA 4) and Travel and Tour Operations (NTA 5 and 6).

VHTTI is the only institute under VETA mandated to offer NTA Levels 4 to 6 in both Hospitality and Tourism. Its programmes combine theoretical instruction and practical learning. The Institute continues to strengthen training relevance in response to workplace practices, industry expectations and occupational tools, with proposed digital learning development intended to complement existing practical resources.

Our Vision

To become the center of excellence in the provision of technical and vocational education that meets the highest international standards of the hospitality and tourism industry.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality technical and vocational education, research, and consultancy services that respond to the needs and challenges of the hospitality and tourism sector.

Main Functions

  • Conduct vocational and technical long courses.
  • Provide generic and tailored short courses in hospitality and tourism.
  • Engage in applied research and development in related disciplines.
  • Provide consultancy to individuals, private businesses and government entities.
  • Provide certification as approved by VETA and NACTVET.
  • Perform other functions stipulated under the VET Act, 1994.

Training Programmes

VHTTI offers training at NTA Levels 4–6 across five principal programme areas:

Culinary Arts (CA)
Food and Beverage Service and Sales (FBSS)
Rooms Division (RD)
Tour Guiding (TG)
Travel and Tour Operations (TTO)
